# Test plan: rain measured at a gauge, counted to the morning sample (candidate version 0.2)

Written 22 November 2026, before any of the results below were computed. The
commit that adds this file is the record of when it was set.

## Question

Version 0.1 uses rain from a weather model (ERA5 through Open-Meteo), counted
over whole calendar days: the day of the test and the two days before. The
county samples the ocean at about 7:30 in the morning, so rain later on the
test day cannot reach that sample, and the model's rain reads well below
nearby gauges in storms. Does rain measured at a nearby gauge, counted over
the 72 hours before the morning sample, forecast the county's test better?

## Data

- The same 243 county tests at the creek mouth (station LM5), 3 January 2022
  to 19 October 2026, used to build version 0.1 (`open-data/rule_v0_1_worked.csv`).
- A test fails above 104 enterococcus per 100 mL; "far over" is above 312.
- The ground-wetness index G is unchanged from version 0.1 (through the day
  before), so only the rain input differs between the candidates.

## Candidates

Each uses the same equation as version 0.1, with its own rain input R:
z = b0 + b1 ln(1 + R) + b2 log10(max(G, 1)), P(fail) = 1 / (1 + e^(-z)).

- **A, version 0.1 (baseline).** R = model rain on the test day and the two
  days before, midnight to midnight.
- **B, gauge.** R = the three daily reports of the CoCoRaHS volunteer gauge
  "Pacifica 0.3 W" (US1CASM0001) dated the test day and the two days before.
  Each report covers the 24 hours to about 7am, so together they cover the
  72 hours before the sample. A missing report is filled, in this order, from
  "Pacifica 1.9 S" (US1CASM0033) for the same date, then from model hourly
  rain for the same 24 hours. The number of fills is reported.
- **C, hourly model.** R = model hourly rain summed over the 72 hours ending
  at 07:00 Pacific on the test day.

## How they are scored

- Each year from 2022 to 2026 is hidden in turn; the equation is refitted on
  the other four years and scored on the hidden one; the hidden-year results
  are pooled. The main score is the ranking score (area under the ROC curve)
  for "fail". Also reported: the ranking score for "far over", the Brier
  score, and the forward-in-time check (2024, 2025 and 2026 each predicted
  from earlier years only).
- All three candidates go through the same code.
- Also reported for each: its weights with 90% ranges, and the highest rain
  value in the fitted tests.

## Rule for adopting a candidate (set now)

- A candidate qualifies if its pooled hidden-year ranking score for "fail" is
  at least 0.02 above version 0.1's. If both qualify, the higher one.
- The 0.02 margin is the one set for version 0.1. It is a judgement about how
  large a gain justifies a change, not a test of statistical significance.
- Section 9 of the paper also says a gain must show in the daily forecast
  log. A qualifying candidate is therefore logged alongside version 0.1 from
  the day it qualifies. When it replaces version 0.1 is the author's
  decision, and will be recorded in this file.
- If no candidate qualifies, version 0.1 stays, and the result is reported
  here all the same.

## Results

Run on 22 November 2026, after this plan was committed. The code that
produced version 0.1's published scores was first run on the frozen file and
gave the same numbers (ranking score 0.726 for "fail" and 0.795 for "far
over", Brier 0.202; forward in time 0.712 and 0.755). All three candidates
then went through that code, on the same 243 tests and the same G.

| | A, version 0.1 | B, gauge | C, hourly model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hidden year: ranking score, fail | 0.726 | 0.710 | 0.722 |
| Hidden year: ranking score, far over | 0.795 | 0.780 | 0.783 |
| Hidden year: Brier score | 0.202 | 0.206 | 0.203 |
| Forward in time: ranking score, fail | 0.712 | 0.714 | 0.722 |
| Forward in time: ranking score, far over | 0.755 | 0.732 | 0.741 |
| b0 (90% range) | 2.05 (1.44 to 2.83) | 1.88 (1.27 to 2.63) | 2.06 (1.43 to 2.84) |
| b1, rain (90% range) | 0.77 (0.52 to 1.12) | 0.62 (0.41 to 0.91) | 0.73 (0.50 to 1.06) |
| b2, ground (90% range) | -1.36 (-1.86 to -0.95) | -1.21 (-1.69 to -0.80) | -1.36 (-1.86 to -0.94) |
| Highest rain in the fitted tests | 77.6 mm (18 Dec 2023) | 93.5 mm (25 Nov 2024) | 96.3 mm (3 Jan 2023) |

The 90% ranges come from refitting each equation on 2,000 random re-draws of
the 243 tests (fixed seed), the same re-draws for every candidate. For
version 0.1 they differ slightly from the paper's (0.51 to 1.13 for rain,
-1.87 to -0.98 for ground), whose re-draws are not in the repository.

Model hourly rain is stored on a fixed clock of UTC-7 all year (checked
against the same series downloaded in UTC). It was moved to Pacific time,
daylight saving included, before the 72 hours ending at 07:00 were summed.
Each hourly value is the rain in the hour ending at its time stamp.

Fills for B. Of the 729 daily reports B needs (243 tests, three days each),
"Pacifica 0.3 W" supplied 726. The other 3 were the reports dated 26 to 28
February 2022, for the test of 28 February 2022: missing at both gauges, so
they came from model hourly rain (0 mm). None came from "Pacifica 1.9 S".
"Pacifica 0.3 W" has one accumulated multi-day report in the period (30
December 2024, with 26 December marked as included in it); neither date falls
in a window B uses.

Verdict. No candidate qualifies. The rule needs a ranking score for "fail" of
at least 0.746 (version 0.1's 0.726 plus 0.02). B scores 0.710, 0.016 below
version 0.1; C scores 0.722, 0.004 below. Version 0.1 stays. B and C are
slightly ahead on the forward-in-time score for "fail" (0.714 and 0.722
against 0.712), but behind on every other score, and the rule rests on the
hidden-year score.

The gauge reads higher than the model overall (1,093 mm against 899 mm summed
over the 243 windows; where both show at least 1 mm, the gauge is typically
1.23 times the model), and the two also disagree sharply on single storms:
for the test of 18 December 2023 the model has 77.6 mm and the gauge 16.8 mm,
and for 25 November 2024 the gauge has 93.5 mm and the model 14.4 mm.
