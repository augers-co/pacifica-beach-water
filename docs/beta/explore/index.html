Follow the water

Six test sites, from the top of town to the surf, numbered downstream. The colour shows how often each one fails.
Map © OpenStreetMap contributors. Markers 4 to 6 are nudged apart; the three are within 200 yards of each other.
Count tests from
Tests that fail under 25% 25 to 74% 75 to 87% 88% or more the limit, 104
The big number is the typical result: the geometric mean of enterococcus per 100 mL, the one measure taken at all six places, July 2024 to September 2026. A test fails when it is over 104.

The result jumps between Sanchez Adobe (2) and Peralta Bridge (3). Two hundred yards up the beach (6) it is about a tenth of the one in the surf at the mouth (5). That describes one spot at the moments it was tested. It does not show the water there is safe.

Whose work this is

San Mateo County Environmental Health
1,265 weekly tests in the ocean at the creek mouth since 2000, and 536 in the creek since 2015.
Surfrider San Mateo County, Blue Water Task Force
475 tests at the creek mouth since December 2013, the only record of the creek from before the highway bridge was rebuilt.
Linda Mar Water Quality Coalition
451 results from four places along the creek, every week since July 2024.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by San Mateo County, the City of Pacifica or any public agency, and naming a group here does not mean that group endorses it.
