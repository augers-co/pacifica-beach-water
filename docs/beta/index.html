Why it never says “safe”

About 4 in 10 tests here come back under the limit. The forecast can say when that is less likely. It cannot say a day is safe: even at the lowest level 1 test in 3 fails, and a test is one sample at one moment.

This forecast is an estimate from past test results and weather. It is not a measurement, an official advisory or medical advice. Official beach postings come from San Mateo County Environmental Health.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by San Mateo County, the City of Pacifica or any public agency, and naming a group here does not mean that group endorses it. The forecast never indicates that the water is safe. It comes with no guarantee of accuracy, and the decision to enter the water is your own.
